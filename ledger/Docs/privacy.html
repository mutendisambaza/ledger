Privacy Policy

Ledger is a personal finance app that reads Gmail receipts to track spending. This policy describes how we handle your data.

Gmail access

We request read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). We only look for messages that appear to be receipts (e.g. subject or sender hints). We do not read, store, or transmit the full content of your emails beyond what is needed to extract purchase amounts and dates.

Storage

Parsed amounts, dates, and merchant names are stored locally on your device. We do not send this data to our servers.

OAuth and Google

Sign-in uses Google OAuth 2.0. We do not receive your Google password. You can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be at this URL.
