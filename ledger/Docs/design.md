# Ledger Design System

## Colors

- **background**: `#0B0B0C`
- **card**: `#171717`
- **cardHover**: `#1C1C1E`
- **text**: `#FAFAFA`
- **textMuted**: `#A1A1AA`
- **accent**: `#3B82F6`

## Typography

- **Primary**: SF Pro (system)
- **Monospace**: SF Mono (amounts)
- **Amounts**: right-aligned, tabular figures

## Spacing

- **xs**: 4pt
- **sm**: 8pt
- **md**: 16pt
- **lg**: 24pt
- **xl**: 32pt

## Components

- **Cards**: cornerRadius 12, subtle shadow
- **Buttons**: minimal, no heavy borders
- **Lists**: clean separators, no chevrons unless actionable

## Tone

- Passive, observational
- No celebrations, no alarms
- "Ledger noticed..." not "Great job!"
